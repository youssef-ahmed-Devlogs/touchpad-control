# Changelog

## 3.1.0

- Added an optional notification-area icon, off by default.
- Added a tray menu to open the app, toggle the touchpad, or exit.
- Persisted the tray setting and localized its menu in English and Arabic.
- Corrected the startup switch display when saving its setting fails.
- Verified fresh defaults, preference persistence, on/off behavior, clean reinstallation, and uninstall restoration.

## 3.0.1

- Corrected Arabic shortcut-key alignment while retaining left-to-right key order.
- Checked Arabic and English layouts in light and dark themes at minimum and default window sizes.

## 3.0.0

- Introduced the redesigned desktop interface.
- Added Light, Dark, and System appearance modes.
- Retained bilingual controls, customizable shortcuts, background startup, and touchpad restoration during uninstall.
